# Pre-Launch Checklist (non-code)

Things that need to happen outside the codebase before real money/customers, most of which were explicitly deferred or handled separately by the founder during planning. Nothing here blocks starting to build — see [[Tracker]] for that — but several block *charging real customers*.

## Legal & compliance
- [ ] Register business (Ghana RGD — sole proprietorship or limited company)
- [ ] Draft Terms of Service
- [ ] Draft Privacy Policy
- [ ] Register with Ghana Data Protection Commission (Act 843 — mandatory, enforcement tightening)
- [ ] Confirm business bank account for Paystack payouts
- [ ] TIN registered (needed for Paystack/aggregator onboarding)

## Branding & identity
- [ ] Product name decided
- [ ] Domain name purchased
- [ ] App icon / favicon / PWA splash screen
- [ ] Final accent color locked in (currently: sunlight-readable palette direction set, exact hex TBD — see [[Design-Language]])

## Payments setup
- [ ] Paystack Ghana merchant account approved (business registration + settlement bank account + Ghana Card required)
- [ ] Test a real MoMo transaction end-to-end before pilot

## Go-to-market
- [ ] Marketing landing page live (see [[Tracker]] Phase 1 #15)
- [ ] Customer acquisition channel decided (direct outreach / WhatsApp groups / referrals from existing contacts — see [[Open-Risks]], previously dismissed)
- [ ] First 2-3 pilot shops identified (founder mentioned informal conversations already started)

## Pricing validation
- [ ] Confirm GHS 99-150/month target against real pilot shop-owner reactions (recalibrated from research — see [[Research-Notes]])

## Support readiness
- [ ] WhatsApp Business number set up for support
- [ ] Founder available/committed to hands-on pilot support (research explicitly recommends this be the founder personally, not delegated — it doubles as product research)
